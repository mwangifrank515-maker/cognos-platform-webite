        Pricing

        Pay per sale, or pay a flat monthly fee

        Your plan is agreed when your account is activated.

        Per transaction

        A small percentage of each payment is deducted before your payout. No monthly bill, so you pay only when you earn.

        Flat monthly

        One fixed fee each month, billed to you as a platform invoice. Payments are paid out in full. Suits high-volume schools and hospitals.

        Safaricom charges

        M-Pesa’s own transaction charges are separate from the Cognos Pay fee and set by Safaricom.

        Security & compliance

        Built for money and for personal data

        Patient, tenant, and student records need more care than a till roll. Here is what protects them.

        Role-based access

        Every staff account gets only the permissions its role needs. A cashier sees their own sales; a teacher sees only their classes.

        Guarded payouts

        Only the owner can change the payout Till or Paybill, and only after re-entering their password. Payouts are recorded once and never repeated.

        Verified sign-ins

        Phone codes verify staff, students, and parents. Repeated wrong codes lock the attempt. “Log out everywhere” ends all sessions.

        Safe key handling

        Passwords and API keys are stored hashed, never in plain text. Sensitive values are redacted from logs.

        Audit trail

        Schools get an audit log of fee adjustments, approvals, and record changes. Big changes need a second person to approve.

        Data Protection Act, 2019

        Cognos Pay acts as data processor for your records. Hospitals can record their KMPDC data handler certificate in the dashboard.

        Questions

        Before you sign up

        Do my customers need an app?

        No. They get an M-Pesa prompt on their phone, or open a link or QR code. Patients, tenants, students, and parents use a web portal in their browser.

        Where does the money go?

        To the Till or Paybill you register, after your fee. You can follow every payout from the dashboard.

        Which businesses can use it?

        Shops and storefronts, hospitals and clinics, landlords and property managers, and schools from primary to college. Hotels, restaurants, salons, gyms, pharmacies, and hardware stores use the same payments toolkit.

        Can staff work without internet?

        In school Registry apps, quick admissions and department messages are saved on the computer and sync when the connection returns. Fees and payments always need a connection.

        Can parents and students see results?

        Yes, through the school portal. Parents see only their own children, and marks appear only after they are verified and locked by the exams office.

        How do I get started?

        Register your business, choose its type, and the dashboard is set up for you. The team then activates your account.
